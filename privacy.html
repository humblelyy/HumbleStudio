PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What we collect

HUMBLE STUDIO does not require an account to browse the public site. The admin area uses Supabase Authentication for authorized administrators. Tool images and tool metadata entered through the admin area are stored in the connected Supabase project.

Cookies and local storage

The public site uses browser storage only to remember that the cookie notice has been dismissed. If optional analytics or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

External links

Tool pages and social links can lead to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by HUMBLE STUDIO.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the HUMBLE STUDIO website.
